Privacy

This is a prototype. Your account is handled by Supabase Authentication and your mood and journal entries are stored in a database tied to your account. Chat messages are sent to our server and to the Groq API to generate replies.

Do not share information you would not want processed by these services. We cannot promise confidentiality beyond what this implementation does. A full policy will be added before any public launch.
